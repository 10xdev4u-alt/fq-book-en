### I. Why can't the chrome browser use Googlegemini?

Before trying out complex network settings, we need to understand a browser character that 90% of users ignore - the QUIC protocol. When you found the proxy turned on, **gemini was not able to use **, most likely because the QUIC agreement, which was opened by Chrome by default, bypassed your proxy tunnel.

#### What is QUIC?

QUIC (Quick UDP Internet Networks) is a UDP-based transmission protocol developed by Google. It was originally intended to make the web pages loaded faster and to enhance user experience by reducing delays in the establishment of connections.

#### Why would it “sell” you?

Most common proxy tools are mainly for TCP traffic containment and encryption. However, QUIC is based on UDP. If your proxy configuration does not force the taking over of all UDP traffic, or if the browser considers the direct connection to be faster, the flow generated by the QUIC protocol may bypass the proxy and send it directly from your local network to the Google server.

And that's when Google sees not the IP of your proxy server, but the real IP of your local operator. That's why it's still necessary to address the root causes of the failure of the chrome browser to use Googlegemini** after a computer goes over the wall.

### III. Progress Check: potential leaks from WebRTC and IPv6

If the situation **gemini cannot open** after the QUIC agreement has been banned, then we need to examine further the other “fish leaks”.

#### Block the WebRTC leak

WebRTC is a technology that supports browsers in real-time voice or video conversations, but it has a well-known side effect: easy to divulge a user ' s real LAN IP and public network IP.

- ** Solution: ** Search and install the "WebRTC Leak Prevent" type plugin at the Chrome Applied Shop, or tick "WebRTC block" in your proxy software settings.

#### 2. Disable IPv6

Many proxy nodes currently support IPv4 only. If your local network turns on IPv6, Chrome might give priority to trying through IPv6 straight to Google. Your real position was immediately exposed because the proxy did not take over the IPv6 traffic.

- ** Solution: ** In the network adaptor settings of the operating system, delete the ticking "Internet Protocol Version 6" (TCP/IPv6).
